Global Shark Attack Incidents

From 2015-2020, there have been 568 cases of shark attacks recorded all around the world.
More than 1/2 of the global fatal attacks were caused by Tiger sharks.


The second most fatal species is the White shark followed by the Bull shark. Whilst the Tiger shark is most commonly found in United States, specifically in Hawaii, the white shark is generally encountered in California, South Africa and Western Australia.
Australia saw the most fatal attacks at 14 victims from 2015 to 2020.

Dataset provided by Brenda Griffith from https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/thedevastator/global-shark-attack-incidents/data
